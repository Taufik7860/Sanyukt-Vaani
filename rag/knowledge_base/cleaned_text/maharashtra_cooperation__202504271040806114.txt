No. Sanini 1524/ C.R.110/13C.—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (2)
of section 165 of the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 (Mah. XXIV of 1961), and of all
other powers enabling it in that behalf, the Government of Maharashtra hereby, makes the following
rules further to amend the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961, after the same having
been previously published as required by sub-section (3) of section 165 of the said Act, namely:—
1. These rules may be called the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Rules,
2025.
2. In the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961 (hereinafter referred to as “the
principal Rules”) after rule 53, the following rules shall be inserted, namely:—
“53A. Establishment of Co-operative District Cadre Employment Fund and Manner of
annual contribution to be made to the fund.—
(1) The District Supervision Co-operative Society shall open an account in the name
of the “Co-operative District Cadre Employment Fund” established by it under sub-section
(4) of section 69A of the Act, in the District Central Co-operative Bank.
(2) The rate of contribution payable to the Co-operative District Cadre Employment
Fund under sub-section (5) of section 69A shall be as follows, namely:—
(a) The Primary Agricultural Credit Society shall on the basis of the previous
years annual salary of the Secretary deputed by the District Supervision Co-operative
Society, after deducting share of District Central Co-operative Bank in the contribution,
contribute to the Co-operative District Cadre Employment Fund fifteen months’ salary
and other payable allowances to the Secretary:
Provided that, if the District Supervision Co-operative Society has nominated
one secretary on more than one Primary Agricultural Credit Societies, then the above
contribution shall be divided amongst those societies based upon their financial
position.
(b) All District Central Co-operative Banks, excluding the Mumbai District
Central Co-operative Bank Limited shall deposit the contribution in the Co-operative
District Cadre Employment Fund at the rate of 0.15 per cent. of short term agricultural
and non-agricultural loans disbursed through Primary Agricultural Credit Societies
and which are outstanding as on 30th September each year and that of medium term
loans, disbursed through Primary Agricultural Credit Societies, due for recovery as
on the 1st October in each year:
Provided that, the District Central Co-operative banks having short term
agricultural loans disbursed through Primary Agricultural Credit Societies more than
that of non-agricultural loans shall not be required to consider non-agricultural loans
for the purpose of such contribution.
(3) The contribution to be paid to the Co-operative District Cadre Employment Fund
shall be paid from the own funds in lump-sum or in monthly or quarterly instalments
depending on the financial position of the Primary Agricultural Credit Society or the District
Central Co-operative Bank, as the case may be. While paying the contribution, the society
or the bank will take into consideration the two per cent. interest margin available on crop

53B. Recruitment of Secretaries.—
The District Supervision Co-operative Society shall recruit secretaries by nomination primarily
from amongst the secretaries appointed by Primary Agricultural Credit Societies with prior permission
of District Level Committee or by direct recruitment. The Government may provide general or
special guidelines for total number of secretaries to be appointed, their educational qualification and
experience, recruitment and nomination procedure and service conditions.”.
